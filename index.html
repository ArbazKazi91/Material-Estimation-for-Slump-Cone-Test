Calculation Formula:

Slump cone volume is calculated using the frustum equation:

V = πh/12 × (D₁² + D₁D₂ + D₂²)

Where:
D₁ = Top diameter of slump cone
D₂ = Bottom diameter of slump cone
h = Height of slump cone

Material quantity:
Material Required = Material Content × Concrete Volume

Wastage is applied to the final material quantity.

About the Calculator

This Slump Cone Test Material Estimator provides a quick estimation of cement, water, fine aggregate and coarse aggregate required for conducting a specified number of slump cone tests.

The calculator is intended for laboratory planning, concrete material estimation and educational purposes.

Standard Slump Cone Dimensions:

  • Top Diameter = 100 mm
  • Bottom Diameter = 200 mm
  • Height = 300 mm
