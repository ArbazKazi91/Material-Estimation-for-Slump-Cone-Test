Calculation Method

Step 1 – Dry Volume:
The volume of the slump cone is calculated as:

V = πh/12 × (D₁² + D₁D₂ + D₂²)

Step 2 – Increase Dry Volume by 54%:
Revised Volume = Dry Volume × 1.54

Step 3 – Apply Wastage:
Estimation Volume = Revised Volume × (1 + Wastage/100)

Step 4 – Material Volume:
The estimation volume is divided according to the entered cement : fine aggregate : coarse aggregate proportion.

Step 5 – Material Quantity:
Material Quantity = Material Volume × Material Density

About the Calculator

This calculator estimates the quantity of cement, fine aggregate and coarse aggregate required for a slump cone test.

The slump cone geometrical volume is initially considered as the dry volume. The dry volume is then increased by 54% to obtain the revised volume.

The specified wastage percentage is subsequently applied to the revised volume, and the resulting volume is used for material estimation.
